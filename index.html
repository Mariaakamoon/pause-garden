PREFERENCES

Settings & Safeguards

Default Session Duration

Pre-select 3, 4, or 5 minutes for guided sessions.

Gentle Motion (Reduced Animations)

Softens or stops moving illustrations and floating orbs.

Screen Break Audio Chimes

Plays a singing bell tone when screen break steps advance so you can look away.

Data & Privacy Guarantee

Pause Garden stores 100% of your notes, reflections, check-in mood, and session counts inside your local web browser using localStorage. No telemetry, no ads, and no external tracking.
